Dosage evidence at a glance
Dose–response — higher dose, bigger effect
25 mg is the effective single dose; benefit sustained to 43 days. Goodwin/COMPASS PMID 36322843 · Raison 37651119.
Potency varies — dose from the COA, not the strain
Strain name ≠ dose (J Fungi 2026). Baeocystin → norpsilocin can add activity — a COA-panel question (Sherwood 2020, J Nat Prod).
Flat 25 mg anchor, individualized on clinical factors — not mg/kg (saturable 5-HT2A, weight-agnostic). Evidence-labeled; not medical advice.
Clinician education — mechanism of action, weight-agnostic dosing & CME
Mechanism of action

Psilocybin is a prodrug, rapidly dephosphorylated to psilocin (the active metabolite). Psilocin is a 5-HT2A receptor agonist; the therapeutic/subjective effect is driven by 5-HT2A receptor occupancy in the brain, which is saturable — it does not scale linearly with body mass.

Why weight-agnostic (not mg/kg)

PK/PBPK data show psilocin exposure varies widely between individuals in ways body weight doesn't predict (Dahmane PMID 32250059; Thaoboonruang 40258947). The field moved from mg/kg to a fixed 25 mg (COMPASS COMP360) because weight-based dosing didn't improve outcomes and flat dosing is reproducible (Goodwin 36322843). Only von Rotz (0.215 mg/kg) & Sloshower (0.3 mg/kg) used mg/kg.

Regulatory implication: flat dosing → standardized product units, clean COA labeling (7.35.2.19), simpler dispensing, and §9 mg/session reporting. With no per-patient compounding, diversion risk is substantially reduced and chain-of-custody stays clean.

Two products, one engine — a govtech and a regtech

Govtech — sold to the state

The department-designated systems of record: licensing & applications, product traceability, patient enrollment, §9 de-identified reporting, and the annual producer/patient audit. Customer: NM DOH (CO: DNM + DORA). Model: state designation + licensee-funded fees. In NM: licensing runs on the incumbent state vendor’s portal today; traceability & enrollment are open.

Regtech — sold to the licensees

Operator compliance software that integrates with the state rail (API / HIPAA-business-associate upload): producers, labs, certifying clinicians, practitioners, facilitators, healing centers. Customer: the licensees. Model: per-seat SaaS (~$150–$600/mo est.) + services.
One rule-pack engine powers both: the regtech generates the data the govtech aggregates, and the §9 outcomes registry (Parakeet) feeds both. Regtech = reliable revenue now; a govtech designation = the larger, defensible prize.
