# Parakeet

**New Mexico Medical Psilocybin Program — govtech with regtech awareness.**

Easy, simple compliance software for the NM Medical Psilocybin Act (SB219):

- **Role-friendly** for applicants, producers, providers, and patients
- **Compliance help** incl. lab health & safety; **minimizes provider & patient burden**
- A **Secretary / regulator dashboard**, **Advisory Board & committee** support, and an **upcoming-events calendar**
- Grounded in **adopted 7.35.2** (producers + labs) and **draft 7.35.3** (patients/clinicians/facilitators/healing centers) NMAC
- A related clinical EHR (*Psilocybin Parakeet*) is a separate application, out of scope here.

Prototype — citations are for traceability, **not** legal or medical advice. Confirm against current rule text.
